=== TEXTE 1 — Description of the feature ===
MotoRoute is a safety app for motorcycle riders in Algeria. Its core safety feature is automatic crash detection with an emergency SMS. The rider turns crash detection on (it is off by default) and saves one or two emergency phone numbers on their emergency card. While crash detection is on and the app is open on screen (typically on the handlebar mount), a crash is detected when the motorcycle was moving at 15 km/h or more in the previous 90 seconds, then an impact above 4 g with the phone tilting over 35° (or above 6 g), then no movement. Without recent GPS speed (e.g. in a tunnel), only an impact above 8 g with tilt counts. The app then shows an alert dialog with a repeating beep and a large "I'm fine — cancel" button for 2 minutes. If the rider does not respond, the app sends one short alert message to those saved numbers only: the rider's name, motorcycle (if it fits in one SMS), GPS coordinates and, only if the rider gave explicit consent for their health data, blood type, for example "MotoRoute SOS: CRASH Rider (bike) blood O+. GPS 36.12345,4.12345. No answer 2 min". SEND_SMS is the only SMS-related permission in the app and it is used for two things only: (1) this automatic alert; (2) a test SMS that the rider starts and confirms, sent to the first saved emergency number ("MotoRoute test SMS. If you read this, your friend's emergency SMS works."). Test mode: the in-app help ("How does it work?" → "Test mode: try the full alert") tells every rider how to show a "Simulate a crash" button (7 quick taps on the "My account" title) to check the whole alert without crashing. It runs the same flow (2-minute countdown, cancel button, one SMS) and that SMS starts with "TEST not a real crash". Test mode is already turned on for the review account, so the reviewer does not need the 7 taps. Everything else opens another app and never uses SEND_SMS: the red SOS button, the WhatsApp and call buttons open the rider's own SMS, WhatsApp or phone app, pre-filled; after a false alarm the app only offers a pre-filled SMS (sms: link) that the rider sends from their own SMS app. Club features (rides, groups, classifieds) never use SMS. The app never reads, receives or stores SMS, never sends SMS to any number the rider did not enter, and is limited in code to 6 SMS per 10 minutes.

=== TEXTE 3 — User disclosure and control ===
Crash detection and the emergency SMS are off by default. The SMS permission is only requested after a user action: "My emergencies" → "Allow SMS", or when the rider turns on crash detection. Before Android's permission screen, a dialog explains that the SMS permission is used only to send one alert SMS to the emergency numbers the rider saved, if they crash and do not answer for 2 minutes (and for the test SMS "MotoRoute test SMS..." that they start themselves), what the SMS contains, and that the app never reads SMS. The dialog has two buttons, "I agree" and "Not now"; "Not now" does not open Android's permission screen and the rider can ask again later from "My emergencies". The blood type is included only if the rider ticks a separate health-data consent box (unticked by default). When saving an emergency number, the rider ticks "I told this contact". The rider sees on screen exactly which numbers will be alerted, can cancel during the 2-minute countdown, and can turn the feature off in one tap. After a false alarm, no SMS is sent automatically; the app only offers a pre-filled SMS that the rider sends from their own SMS app. The privacy policy describes the emergency SMS: https://elaidremili-sudo.github.io/Elaid/confidentialite.html
