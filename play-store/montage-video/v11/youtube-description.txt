DESCRIPTION :
Demonstration video for the Google Play SMS permissions declaration of MotoRoute, a safety app for motorcycle riders in Algeria (core functionality: physical safety / emergency alert).

Real screen recordings of two phones: the rider's phone and the emergency contact's phone. Test build, test account. Waiting times are cut (no step removed), fast parts are marked ×4 ×8 ×10 ×16, no sound. Personal data (phone number, GPS position, map, names) is blurred. Step 1 was recorded separately (20:40) from a fresh state, after resetting the SMS permission in Android settings; steps 2–9 are one take filmed just before (20:16–20:32).

SEND_SMS is used for two things only:
1. The automatic crash alert: ONE SMS to each number the rider saved (2 max), after 2 minutes without an answer. Crash detection is off by default and only runs while MotoRoute is open on screen. In this video the alert is started by the test-mode button "Simulate a crash" (explained in the app's help, "Test mode: try the full alert"): same flow, and the SMS starts with "TEST not a real crash".
2. The test SMS ("MotoRoute test SMS…") that the rider starts and confirms.
MotoRoute never reads, receives or stores SMS. The SOS, WhatsApp and call buttons open the rider's own apps, pre-filled. After a false alarm nothing is sent automatically: the rider can send a pre-filled message from their own SMS app.
MotoRoute does not replace emergency services.
